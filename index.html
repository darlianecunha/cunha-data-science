Vessel Efficiency Predictor
vessel-efficiency-app.vercel.app
TRL 6
Purpose

Predicts a ship's operational CO₂ efficiency grade (A to E) from its structural attributes alone, with the CO₂ fields that define the grade deliberately excluded to avoid target leakage. Gradient boosting roughly doubles the baseline accuracy on 1,570 EU MRV vessels, with deadweight and EEXI/EIV as the strongest predictors.

How to use

Pick a ship type and a real vessel from the EU MRV dataset and compare the predicted grade with the actual one. The full scikit-learn pipeline is on GitHub.

Who it is for

Fleet managers, maritime data scientists and researchers screening vessels for decarbonisation.

Efficiency gain

Flags likely low-efficiency ships before operational emissions data are available.

BluePort AI
blue-port-ia.vercel.app
TRL 6
Purpose

A computer-vision classifier that sorts port and marine waste into six recycling streams from a single photo. It uses a frozen CLIP ViT-B/32 model with a linear probe, reaching 94.6% cross-validated accuracy on 11,451 images. It grew out of an Omdena AI project on the Ganges river.

How to use

Upload a photo on the website, where the model runs entirely in the browser, or send it to the Telegram bot @blueportbot. Both return the category, a confidence score and disposal guidance in seconds.

Who it is for

Port environmental teams, dock workers and computer-vision researchers.

Efficiency gain

Puts instant, no-training waste classification in any worker's hands, with no server and no data upload.

Port Emissions MCP Server
github.com/darlianecunha/port-emissions-mcp
TRL 5
Purpose

A Model Context Protocol server that lets Claude query Brazilian port statistics (247 installations, 2010 to 2026) and estimate at-berth CO₂ with the IMO method, citing source and method in every answer.

How to use

Install the server in Claude Desktop or Claude Code and ask questions in plain language, such as cargo trends for a port or the CO₂ of a berthing.

Who it is for

Analysts, consultants and researchers who work with port data through AI assistants.

Efficiency gain

Turns official port statistics and a validated emissions method into answers an assistant can give and justify.

Shipping Carbon Costs · EU ETS Ranking
shipping-carbon-costs.vercel.app
TRL 7
Purpose

Ranks 3,303 shipping companies by their EU ETS carbon cost, built on the verified EU MRV 2024 dataset (12,610 ships, 87.3 Mt of ETS-scoped CO₂). At €80 per allowance the maritime bill reaches €6.99 billion a year, with the top 10 companies carrying 21.5% and MSC alone facing about €427 million.

How to use

Read the headline figures and the top-20 chart, then consult the ranking table of the top 25 Document-of-Compliance holders.

Who it is for

Shipping companies, maritime economists, policy analysts and researchers on EU ETS exposure.

Efficiency gain

Converts the EU MRV dataset into a company-level carbon-cost ranking: who actually pays for shipping's carbon.

Brazil Vessel Call Intelligence
brazil-vessel-call-intelligence.vercel.app
TRL 6
Purpose

IMO-based analytics for Brazilian ports: 30,972 port calls and 8,506 vessels (2019 to 2025) at Itaguaí, Itaqui and Paranaguá, with 99.1% deadweight coverage, expanding to the top 20 ports. Browse the modules or search any vessel by name or IMO number to see its calls, size and estimated at-berth emissions. English and Portuguese.

Who it is for

Port authorities, terminal operators, investors and researchers in maritime decarbonisation.

Efficiency gain

The only public source of harmonised vessel-level port-call and emissions data for Brazilian ports.

SDG Port Hub · SDG Attributes
sdg-port-hub-sable.vercel.app
TRL 8
Purpose

The interactive version of a peer-reviewed port sustainability framework: 84 indicators across 8 SDGs and 20 thematic dimensions (Cunha et al., 2025, Marine Policy). It won 1st place, Academic Production, at the 2026 Inova Itaqui Prize and is the framework adopted for the update of Brazil's port environmental performance index (IDA/ANTAQ).

How to use

Consult the indicator reference, then score each indicator; the overall score and performance band update automatically, in English or Portuguese.

Who it is for

Port sustainability managers, regulators, researchers and auditors assessing SDG performance.

Efficiency gain

A ready, peer-reviewed assessment model with instant self-scoring for reporting and gap analysis.

Shipping Methane Monitor
shipping-methane-monitor.vercel.app
TRL 7
Purpose

The first open observatory of the verified CH₄ and N₂O emissions now reported under EU MRV (2024 and 2025). On the same 11,453 ships, verified CO₂ fell 5.1% while CH₄ rose 21.2%. In 2025, 406 LNG carriers emitted 59.5% of all verified methane: decarbonisation is trading CO₂ for methane.

How to use

Read the headline findings, explore the charts by ship type and consult the full breakdown table.

Who it is for

Climate policy analysts, maritime researchers and journalists covering LNG and shipping decarbonisation.

Efficiency gain

Turns a 113-column regulatory dataset into the answer to one question: what does CO₂-only accounting miss?

Vessel Gigantism
vessel-gigantism.vercel.app
TRL 7
Purpose

Traces three decades of the "fewer ships, bigger ships" trend in European ports using Eurostat data (mar_tf_qm). Across the EU-27, average container-ship size per call rose 2.3 times (16,892 to 38,575 GT) while arrivals fell 19%, and some ports grew far more, such as Gdansk at 11.8 times.

How to use

Select ports and a ship type to compare average vessel size per call, arrivals and growth multiples, then read the thirty-year ledger.

Who it is for

Port planners, maritime economists and researchers studying fleet consolidation and port capacity.

Efficiency gain

Turns decades of port statistics into an interactive view of one of the sector's defining structural trends.

PDF → VOSviewer Network
text-umber-delta.vercel.app
TRL 7
Purpose

Turns a set of PDFs into a word co-occurrence network for VOSviewer Online, detecting the most frequent terms automatically. Text is extracted entirely in the browser, with Portuguese and English stopwords removed and nothing sent to a server.

How to use

Upload the PDFs, set the network parameters (minimum frequency, co-occurrence and maximum terms) and download the JSON. Then open it at VOSviewer Online.

Who it is for

Researchers running bibliometric or literature reviews and text mining.

Efficiency gain

Automates co-occurrence maps from raw PDFs, a task that is slow and error-prone by hand.

PDF Terms → VOSviewer
textmining2.vercel.app
TRL 5
Purpose

Directed text mining with a controlled vocabulary. You define the keywords and the app counts their occurrences and co-occurrences across your PDFs, with grouped synonyms, compound terms, wildcards and thematic categories, then generates a JSON file for VOSviewer Online.

How to use

Upload the PDFs and list your keywords (group synonyms with "=", use "*" for variants and "[ ]" for categories, or load a domain pack). Download the JSON and open it at VOSviewer Online.

Who it is for

Researchers running directed bibliometric or content analysis with a predefined thesaurus.

Efficiency gain

Measures exactly the concepts the study cares about and maps them, without manual counting.

SDG Radar · Coastal Maranhão
radarods.vercel.app
TRL 3
Purpose

A municipal sustainability platform for the 27 coastal municipalities of Maranhão's Equatorial Margin, adapting the ISM-ODS method first developed for ports. It scores SDGs 7, 11, 13, 14 and 17 on a 1 to 5 maturity scale, with full data for four pilot municipalities. Portuguese interface.

How to use

Read each municipality's radar, ranking and gap to the 2030 target, then adjust indicators in the simulator to see which climate-finance calls the new score would unlock.

Who it is for

Municipal managers, policy analysts and researchers on local SDG progress.

Efficiency gain

Shows in real time how indicator improvements change the index, supporting evidence-based planning.
